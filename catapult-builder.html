How to build

  1. Click a part in the library.
  2. Move it over the build. Dots show where it can snap.
  3. Turn it with R T F until it faces the right way, then click to place it.
  4. Click a placed part to pick it up or remove it.

Drag empty space to look around. Right-drag to slide. Scroll to zoom. The arm stays level while you build.
