How the estimate works

The model estimates wheel speed from motor speed, wheel diameter, and gear reduction. It estimates acceleration from motor choice, a simplified battery factor, gear reduction, and vehicle mass. It then estimates the time needed to cover 30 ft. The battery factors are teaching assumptions, not measured specifications for your exact cells, holder, wiring, or motor.

wheel speed ∝ motor speed × wheel diameter ÷ gear reduction
acceleration ∝ motor factor × battery factor × gear reduction ÷ vehicle mass

This leaves out traction, wheel slip, alignment, friction, wiring losses, and changes in motor performance as the battery drains. In your report, compare the trend you expected with what the simulation showed, and explain why a real vehicle might differ.
