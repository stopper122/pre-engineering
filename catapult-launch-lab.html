Your launcher

Each launcher number shoots a little differently, like real kits. Use the number Mr. Brown gives you.

  1. Launch, then watch it in slow motion (⅛×). Film the screen with your phone if you like.
  2. Step frame by frame. Write down the frame where the arm hits the stop bar (the ball leaves the cup) and the frame where the ball touches the wall.
  3. Time in the air = (hit frame − release frame) ÷ 240.
  4. Use the protractor in the close-up to read the launch angle, and the meter stick to read the release height.

The tape and sticks are in meters. The floor tape starts at 0 right under the ball when it leaves the cup.
