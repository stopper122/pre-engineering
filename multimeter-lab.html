How to use the meter
  • Slide the meter to ON. This meter is manual range: you pick the range. The display shows 1 at the far left when the reading is over range, so turn up one range.
  • Volts: DCV 20 fits this 6 V board. Red lead in VΩmA. Probe across a part with the power on.
  • Amps: pull a jumper (J1–J4) and put the probes on its two posts. Use DCA 200m with the red lead in VΩmA (200 mA max, it has a fuse). For J1 (about 0.4 A) use 5A with the red lead in 5ADC.
  • Ohms: Ω 200, red lead in VΩmA, power off (S1 OFF). Pull jumpers to cut off other paths. This meter has no beeper; a good connection reads close to 0.
  • Put a bulb on the test bench to check it by itself.
